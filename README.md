# aapl-msft-hq-brief

This repository holds the investment committee's research brief for the upcoming West Coast tour of Apple (Cupertino, CA) and Microsoft (Redmond, WA).

## What it's for

- A refreshed pre-trip brief on **Apple (AAPL)** and **Microsoft (MSFT)** covering:
  - Official headquarters locations (coordinates, confirmed street addresses, ground elevation)
  - Corporate actions: latest cash dividends with ex-dividend dates, and latest stock splits with dates and ratios
  - Official visitor centers and their distance from each headquarters
- The brief also documents the driving distance and estimated driving time between the two corporate campuses.
- The detailed research is filed in the issue titled **"HQ site and corporate actions brief: AAPL and MSFT"**, which is assigned to GitHub Copilot so it can start drafting the trip report from the filed research.

## Contents

- `README.md` — this overview
- Issue **"HQ site and corporate actions brief: AAPL and MSFT"** — the full research data: coordinates, confirmed addresses, elevations, visitor centers, driving distance/time between the two HQs, latest dividends with ex-dates, and latest splits with dates and ratios.
